Privacy Policy

Data protection, online payment security, and privacy practices

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Online Payment Portal of Corporate Management of Schools, Archdiocese of Changanacherry. We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of the personal and financial information of our teaching and non-teaching staff members using this portal for online invoice and contribution payments.

2. Information We Collect

When staff members log in and initiate an online payment through the portal, we may collect and process the following details:

  • Staff Identification Details: Name, Staff ID, Designation, School Name, Email Address, and Mobile Number.
  • Invoice & Transaction Information: Invoice number, payment description (such as monthly contribution or one-day salary remittance), invoice amount, payment date, and bank transaction reference numbers.
  • Technical Logs: IP address, browser type, and session timestamps required for security auditing and payment reconciliation.

3. Online Payment Processing & Financial Security

All online payments made through this portal are processed securely via Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized and PCI-DSS compliant bank payment gateways. Corporate Management of Schools does not store your Debit/Credit Card numbers, CVV, Net Banking credentials, or UPI PINs on its servers. All sensitive payment authentication occurs directly on the encrypted bank payment gateway page.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify staff identity and display pending or completed payment invoices.
  • To generate official electronic payment receipts and update institutional accounting records.
  • To resolve payment discrepancies, failed transactions, or refund inquiries with our banking partner.
  • To comply with statutory, audit, and regulatory obligations.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade staff personal information to third parties. Information is shared strictly with our authorized banking/payment gateway partners solely for completing and reconciling online transactions, or when required by law.

6. Refund & Cancellation Policy

In the event of a duplicate deduction or a technical error where the amount is debited from the staff member's bank account without receipt generation, the transaction will be reconciled with the bank payment gateway within 5 to 7 working days and any excess payment will be refunded to the original source account.
